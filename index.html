Plan Before You Build

Course Outline Template

Turn your course idea into a lean outline where every module earns its place. Start with the outcome, map the path, cut the scenery, then walk away with a plan you can build from.

Step 1

Name the outcome

Every strong course starts at the finish line. What will your member be able to do when they complete this course?

Write it as one sentence about their life, in plain words they'd use themselves. This sentence becomes your filter for every decision below.

Step 2

Map the path

List the steps a member takes to travel from where they are now to that outcome. Each step becomes a module.

Most courses run long because their creators keep adding. Your members are time poor and attention poor, so a short path they finish beats a long path they abandon. If you're reaching past six modules, ask whether you're planning one course or three.
Step 3

Run the scenery check

Go back through each module above and tap Step or Scenery. A step moves your member closer to the outcome. Scenery decorates the trip. Anything you mark as scenery lands in your Save for Later list below, so cutting it costs you nothing.

    Step 4

    Give each lesson one job

    Open each surviving module above and list its lessons. For every lesson, name the single job it does. If you catch yourself writing "and" in the job line, you're looking at two lessons.

    Come back to this

    Your judgment sharpens as you build. Put these on your calendar:

    • 1 month: Reread your outline. Have your first members shown you a step you missed or a module they skip?
    • 3 months: Check your Save for Later list. Has anything earned its way back in, or are you glad you cut it?
    • 6 months: Outline your next course without opening this tool. When the outcome-first habit lives in your head, this template has done its job.
